\section*{Figure captions (drafts)}

\paragraph{Figure 1 (main; money\_plot).}
Measured ratio $f(S^{\tilde f}_{\mathrm{greedy}})/f(S^{f}_{\mathrm{greedy}})$
against measured selection error $\etasel$ (log scale), at $K=5$ and $K=30$.
Solid: the exact worst case $\rho_K$ (Theorem~\ref{thm:exact}); dashed: the
guarantee $L_K$ (Proposition~\ref{prop:guarantee}).  Colored points are runs of
the three experiment families (feature selection with cross-validation
surrogates; influence maximization with partially observed graphs; extractive
summarization with heuristic surrogates); black crosses are the constructed
worst-case instances of Theorem~\ref{thm:exact} executed by the same
pipeline, which realize their theoretical values to $10^{-10}$.  Real tasks
sit far above the worst-case curves; the constructed instances sit on them.
The number of points beyond the axis range is annotated.  Ratio denominators
are the true-value greedy (an OPT proxy).

\paragraph{Figure 2 (auxiliary; aux\_eta\_sel\_by\_K).}
Distribution of measured $\etasel$ by budget $K$ for each experiment family
(boxes: quartiles over runs; log scale; fliers suppressed).  Selection error
stabilizes within the first few steps in influence maximization and stays in
the single digits for learned surrogates.

\paragraph{Figure 3 (auxiliary; aux\_p\_vs\_eta).}
Median $\etasel$ at $K=30$ against the edge observation probability $p$ of
the partially observed graph, per network (log scale).  Less observed graphs
produce larger selection error on all four networks; over the same range the
realized ratio falls only from about $0.99$ to $0.88$, while the certified
bound $L_{30}(\etasel)$ falls by two orders of magnitude.  This quantifies
when prediction error matters for this application.

\paragraph{Figure 4 (auxiliary; aux\_d\_dtilde\_scatter).}
True gain $d$ against predicted gain $\tilde d$ over all candidate
evaluations of one feature-selection dataset, with the multiplicative band
of $\eta=2$ shaded.  Sign disagreements concentrate near zero gain, where
the accuracy quantization of the objective dominates; large gains are
predicted with the correct order.  This motivates evaluating guarantees at
$\etasel$ rather than at the band error of every candidate pair.
